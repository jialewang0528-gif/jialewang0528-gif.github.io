Veraitas

Problem
Crypto has a high barrier to entry for people learning to trade.
Proposed solution
A Beginner Mode that explains every number and cost before you trade, and an AI agent that answers in plain English.
  1. Cost check
    Before you buyFee, hidden cost and sell-back value from a live quote.
  2. Market
    Live chart and order bookFrom the True Markets API. Click the chart and the agent explains that move.
  3. Agent
    Claude Haiku with 5 toolssearch_web, get_market, get_portfolio, estimate_buy, prepare_order. Independent calls run in parallel.
  4. Trades
    Real trades, press and hold to confirmSolana and Base, settled in USDC. The agent can prep an order but never place one.
  5. Auto-sell
    Take-profit or stop-lossSet on the buy ticket.
  6. Beginner
    Swing Check and trade receiptsA normal day’s risk in dollars, and a receipt after every fill.

CS2 Integrity Watch

Problem
Match-fixing gets caught late, by someone rewatching hours of video.
Proposed solution
Compare a win-probability model with live betting odds every round, and flag maps where the market moves first.
  1. Data
    1,988 pro maps, 42,009 roundsParsed from demos with demoparser2: money, equipment, armor, buy type, side, score.
  2. Market
    12,489 Polymarket events1-minute prices and every fill.
  3. Align
    One clock for both feedsInferred the 64-tick rate and anchored each map on its market’s settlement.
  4. Model
    Logistic regression, isotonic calibrationMatch-level holdout. Brier 0.178 on 9,163 rounds vs. 0.25 for a coin flip.
  5. Benchmark
    XGBoostWe kept logistic regression: its coefficients explain each alert exactly, and SHAP has no s390x build.
  6. Deploy
    IBM LinuxONE (s390x)Ingest, training, scoring and dashboard on one machine. About 9 ms per score, 33 tests.
  7. Alert
    15+ point gap for 2+ roundsOnly when the market moved first. 474 of 1,014 market-matched maps raise nothing.
